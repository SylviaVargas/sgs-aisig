Discover New Paths in Your Family History with AI

Welcome to the Seattle Genealogical Society’s AI Special Interest Group, a friendly community of genealogists exploring how artificial intelligence can support—and never replace—sound research. Together we test tools, share examples, and learn practical ways AI can make our family history work easier and more enjoyable.

What if you could:

  • Read your great-grandmother’s faded handwriting in minutes instead of hours.
  • Translate foreign-language documents without learning the language first.
  • Organize decades of scattered research notes into clear summaries.
  • Enhance hard-to-see photos so you can recognize faces and details.
  • Get fresh ideas for breaking through brick-wall ancestors.

AI tools are starting to help with all of these tasks. You do not need to be a tech expert—only curious and willing to experiment.

Whether you are struggling with hard-to-read records, overwhelmed by your notes, or simply curious about what AI can and cannot do, this group offers hands-on demonstrations, discussion, and guidance about ethical, responsible use of AI in genealogy. We focus on real examples, not hype.

Join us to learn, share, and discover together.

Have an idea, question, or concern about AI and genealogy? Please share it using our feedback form (link coming soon or provided by SGS).
